Part A — Extend the Four Working Labs

20 points · ≈ 110 minutes

General rules for all four: work on a copy; add a comment line before every line you add; if a lab refuses to run, read the error message bottom-up — it names the line. Each extension ends with Evidence: what to screenshot for your evidence/ folder.

A1 — ERP: 2nd Product + Safe Restocking

5 pts

Goal: prove you understand "one shared database" by growing it.

Add a second product to ERP_DATABASE, keep the silo copy with a deliberately old price, and write restock(product_name, quantity) that adds stock but politely refuses invalid products or non-positive quantities.

Evidence: modified .py + screenshot showing successful restock and the refusal message.

A2 — E-commerce: Subscription Card

5 pts

Goal: turn the revenue-model table into working numbers.

Add SUB_PRICE = 3.00 and a third card "Subscription (digital)" showing MRR = subscribers × $3.00, tiny digital cost, and monthly profit, clearly labeled "per month".

Check: at 50 subscribers → $150.00/mo; at 500 → $1,500.00/mo.

A3 — Knowledge Base: Ranking + Search Fix

5 pts

Goal: operate the knowledge-management value chain, not just recite it.

Write top_articles(n) ranked by usage, and fix the known weakness where "wifi keeps dropping" finds nothing useful.

Evidence: screenshot showing the successful search and the Top-articles list.

A4 — Decision Dashboard: Fixed Cost

5 pts

Goal: feel how fixed costs move a break-even point.

Add a 7th slider "Fixed cost per evening", $0–$60, default $25. Break-even becomes (staff × wage + fixed ÷ hours) ÷ (spend − ingredient cost).

Check: at $0 fixed cost, behaves like the original (break-even ≈ 7.8).

Part B — Five Concepts, Your Words

10 points · ≈ 40 minutes

Answer in 2–4 sentences each, in your own words, always about your capstone company.

  1. Name one realistic information-silo risk in your company and which design idea removes it.
  2. Choose the e-commerce type (B2C / B2B / C2C) and one revenue model, and justify both.
  3. Name one piece of tacit knowledge your company would lose if one person left, and describe capturing it via the four value-chain steps.
  4. Pick one real decision, classify it (structured / semi-structured / unstructured), and name the supporting system (MIS, DSS, or ESS).
  5. GIGO: name one way garbage data could enter your systems and one habit that prevents it.
